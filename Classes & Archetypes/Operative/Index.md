# Operative

#### Technologist’s Exploit
You learn to improvise limited technological powers with the aid of a wristpad. See PHB chapter 10 for the general rules of techcasting and PHB chapter 11 for the tech powers list.

***Tech Powers Known.*** <br> You learn 2 tech powers of your choice. You learn an additional power at 5th, 8th, 11th, 14th, 17th, and 20th level (total of 8). You may not learn a tech power of a level higher than your Max Power Level. You may only learn tech powers with a casting time of 1 action or of 1 minute or longer as well as powers that do not require a weapon attack as part of their casting.

***Tech Points.*** <br>
You have a number of tech points equal to half your Operative level. You use these tech points to cast tech powers. You regain all expended tech points when you finish a short or long rest.

***Max Power Level.*** <br> Many tech powers can be overcharged, consuming more focus points to create a greater effect. You can overcharge these powers to a maximum level, which increases at higher levels. Your Max Power Level is 1st. It increases to 2nd at 8th level and 3rd at 14th level. You may only cast force powers at 3rd-level once. You regain the ability to do so after a long rest.

***Techcasting Ability*** <br> Intelligence is your techcasting ability for your tech powers. You use your Intelligence whenever a power refers to your techcasting ability. Additionally, you use your Intelligence modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a tech power you cast and when making an attack roll with one.
- **Tech save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier
- **Tech attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

***Techcasting Focus*** <br> You use a wristpad (found in PHB chapter 5) as a tech focus for your tech powers.
