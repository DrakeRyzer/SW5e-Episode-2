# Modifiable Items
Below are a list of generic templates for Modifiable Items. These items can accept more Item Modifications than other items.

### Modifiable Blaster
*Weapon (any blaster), <font style="color:lime">premium</font>*
<br> *Requires Attunement*

This modifiable blaster can have four blaster Item Modifications slots installed rather than 2.

Addtionally, if the item is of prototype or advanced rarity it comes with a single vacant Augment Slot and if the item is of legendary or artifact rarity comes with two vacant Augment Slots.
